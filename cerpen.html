Tata Si Tapir 🌸

Ditulis oleh Anis

Saat aku membuka mata, kulihat ada makhluk besar di depanku. Aku tidak tahu itu apa. Lalu kemudian dia berbisik lirih disampingku dan berkata, "Selamat datang di dunia, Anakku Sayang." Ternyata dia adalah Ibuku. Aku tidak tahu kenapa aku bisa mengerti apa yang dia katakan. Aku seolah baru bangun dari tidur yang sangat panjang. Tak lama setelah itu, dia dengan lembut membantuku untuk berdiri. Aku merasa sangat lemah dan bisa jatuh kapan saja. Tapi Ibuku dengan pelan mulai menuntunku untuk berjalan.

Perlahan tapi pasti, akhirnya aku sudah bisa berjalan. Rasanya sudah lebih baik dari saat pertama kali aku berdiri tadi. Ibuku selalu di sampingku dan terus mengajakku berbicara. Katanya, anak-anak lain biasanya butuh waktu lebih lama untuk berjalan setelah dilahirkan. "Kamu memang anak Ibu yang hebat. Setiap hari, selama lebih dari satu tahun kamu dalam perut Ibu, Ibu berdo'a agar kamu jadi anak yang sehat dan kuat."

Tak lama setelah itu, Ibu menyuruhku untuk minum dan menyusu padanya. Rasanya sulit digambarkan tapi aku suka dan ingin terus menyusu karena aku merasa lapar dan haus.

"Ibu, apakah aku benar anakmu?" Tanyaku di sela-sela pembelajaran berjalanku.

"Tentu saja, kenapa kamu tanya begitu?" Balas Ibuku.

"Kenapa warna kulitku terlihat sangat berbeda dibandingkan Ibu?" Tanyaku penasaran dari tadi karena ibuku yang begitu besar, memiliki warna kulit gelap di bagian depan, dan warna yang lebih terang di bagian belakang. Sedangkan aku, juga memiliki kulit gelap dan terang, tapi corak kulitku bergaris-garis lebih rapat.

"Seiring bertambahnya usia, kulitmu akan mengalami banyak perubahan. Dan kelak saat dewasa, kamu memiliki corak yang sama dengan Ibu." Jawab Ibuku sambil tersenyum.

"Benarkah itu, Ibu?" Tanyaku kembali penasaran.

"Ibu tidak mungkin bohong pada anakku yang manis ini." Dia tersenyum kemudian tiba-tiba menatapku dengan riang. "Ibu terpikirkan nama yang cocok untukmu, anakku. Tata. Sangat manis bukan?"

"Aku menyukainya, Bu." Kataku sambil tersenyum.

"Mulai sekarang, kita akan terus sama-sama ya, Tata jangan jauh-jauh dari Ibu, mengerti?"

"Iya. Aku mengerti." Kataku sambil mengangguk.

Hari demi hari berlalu. Ibuku tanpa lelah mengajariku bermacam-macam hal. Mulai dari memilih daun-daun yang bisa dimakan. Mengajariku berenang di sungai yang dangkal. Menjelajah hutan dan memberitahu daerah mana yang sebaiknya tidak aku datangi karena berbahaya. Selain karena rawan makhluk buas, Ibuku bilang ada makhluk berkaki dua yang kerap terlihat di hutan. Ibuku bilang makhluk berkaki dua itu tidaklah seganas makhluk lain, setidaknya pada makhluk seperti kami. Untuk itu Ibu berpesan apabila suatu saat mungkin berpapasan dengan mereka, Ibu menyuruh agar jalan perlahan saja dan abaikan mereka. Kita tidak akan diganggu jika tidak mengganggu mereka. Aku hanya mengangguk tiap kali Ibu menceritakan atau mengajariku sesuatu. Aku tidak terlalu memikirkannya. Toh aku akan terus bersama Ibu. Pasti Ibu yang akan menghadapi makhluk berkaki dua dan terus berada di sampingku. Pikirku.

Hingga tiba waktunya Ibu bilang bahwa sudah saatnya aku mencari pasangan dan wilayah baru untuk nantinya ditinggali oleh keluargaku. Awalnya aku terasa berat harus berpisah dengan Ibuku. Tapi entah kenapa, ada dorongan lain dalam diriku yang menyuruhku untuk mengetahui dan menjelajahi hutan lebih dalam. Aku berpamitan dengan Ibuku yang terlihat makin lemah termakan usia. Tak lupa dia mendoakanku serta menyuruhku agar selalu hati-hati dan waspada dalam setiap langkahku.

Berhari-hari aku menjelajah hutan yang sebelumnya belum pernah aku masuki. Tapi aku belum bertemu dengan makhluk seperti aku yang lain. Hingga dari kejauhan, aku mendengar suara yang asing dan belum pernah aku dengar sebelumnya. Aku mendekati sumber suara itu, hingga semakin dekat dan tiba-tiba saja aku berada di ujung hutan. Jalan tanah yang semula aku injak dengan lembut, kini tiba-tiba berganti jadi tanah keras seperti batu. "Sepertinya aku tersesat dan salah jalan. Sebaiknya aku kembali."

Tapi, saat aku akan membalikkan badan, aku melihat ada benda kotak yang berjalan mendekat lalu berhenti tak jauh dari tempatku. Ada dua makhluk berkaki dua yang keluar dari sana. Aku teringat nasihat Ibuku yang menyuruhku untuk tetap tenang dan abaikan saja jika bertemu mereka. Saat aku mau kembali ke hutan, tiba-tiba banyak benda kotak lainnya yang tiba-tiba muncul diikuti banyak makhluk berkaki dua lainnya. Aku yang semula tenang, kini jadi agak takut karena mereka semua berkerumun mengelilingiku. Ada beberapa di antara mereka yang memegang benda kotak kecil dan mengarahkan padaku seolah sedang melihatku lewat itu.

Aku bingung. Kakiku lemas persis seperti saat aku baru mulai belajar berjalan. Akhirnya aku duduk di atas tanah yang keras itu untuk beberapa saat. Setelah itu aku melihat ada satu makhluk berkaki dua yang mengusir mereka satu per satu hingga mereka cukup jauh dari tempatku berdiam diri. ​Melihat hal itu, aku tidak menyia-nyiakan kesempatan untuk kabur dari sana. Aku berdiri dan mulai berjalan menjauh dari kerumunan makhluk berkaki dua. Tidak ada yang mengikutiku. Sepertinya Ibu benar soal mereka yang tidak jahat khususnya pada makhluk seperti kami. Aku lega, syukurlah.

Cukup jauh aku berjalan, hingga ​akhirnya aku kembali ke dalam hutan yang cukup lebat dengan pepohonan. Aku mencoba menjelajah tiap sudutnya. Aku merasa tempatku sekarang sudah sangat jauh dari tempat makhluk berkaki dua berada tadi. Aku kembali memenuhi rasa lapar dengan bermacam-macam dedaunan hutan. Tak lama berselang, aku mendengar derap langkah yang perlahan mendekat ke arahku. Saat aku hendak berlari karena aku merasakan hawa ancaman, sebuah tongkat panjang menembus perutku. Aku langsung tersungkur dengan rasa sakit yang teramat sangat menjalar di sekujur tubuhku. Aku melihat beberapa makhluk berkaki dua yang sempat kulihat tadi berlarian mendekat padaku yang sedang sekarat.

Aku mencoba sekuat tenaga bangkit untuk berusaha kabur tapi lagi-lagi aku terjatuh dan rasa sakit yang terus bertambah, semakin menyiksaku. Aku pun pasrah saat melihat banyak sekali darah keluar dari perutku. Apakah ini adalah akhir hidupku? pikiran itu semakin menguat saat aku melihat makhluk berkaki dua memegang semacam benda panjang dari logam yang terlihat mampu menebas apapun di depannya. Aku menutup mata di tengah rasa sakit yang menyiksa. "Maafkan aku, karena aku ternyata belum terlalu kuat untuk hidup mandiri tanpamu, Ibu."

Epilog 🌸

Aku melihat makhluk sepertiku terbaring tak berdaya di tengah-tengah makhluk berkaki dua. Tunggu sebentar. Bukankah itu aku? Bukankah aku sudah mati? Kenapa aku masih terbangun dan bisa berdiri lagi? Tapi kakiku lemas. Apakah mereka tidak melihatku? Kenapa mereka mengabaikan "aku"?. Untuk beberapa saat aku bergulat dengan pikiranku sendiri. Sepertinya memang benar bahwa aku sudah mati. Dan "aku" yang sekarang ini mungkin semacam ruh yang terlepas dari jasad "Tata".

Aku bisa melihat bagaimana para makhluk berkaki dua mulai memotong dan mengiris-iris bagian tubuhku. Mereka sesekali tertawa sangat keras entah apa yang lucu dengan mencincang bagian tubuh makhluk lain. Mereka bahkan melakukan itu dengan ekspresi tanpa rasa bersalah. Aku melihat ada yang memegang benda kotak kecil aneh dan mulai berbicara dengan benda itu. Mereka kemudian memasukkan bagian-bagian kecil tubuhku ke dalam wadah berisi air mendidih. Aku baru tersadar ternyata tempat ini sangat berbeda dengan terakhir kali aku jatuh tersungkur.

Aku tidak mampu lagi melihat semua ini. Ternyata Ibu salah. Makhluk berkaki dua sangat kejam dan rakus. Mereka bahkan menyiksaku sebelum benar-benar membunuhku. Mereka merasa sangat senang dengan kekejaman yang dengan sadar mereka lakukan. Sangat rakus hingga memakan seluruh tubuhku tanpa sisa.

Aku berjalan perlahan tak tahu arah tujuan. Cahaya terang di ujung jalan begitu menyilaukan. Aku tanpa sadar melangkah menuju tempat cahaya itu berasal. Dari kejauhan aku melihat wajah-wajah yang serupa tapi tampak asing seperti belum pernah aku lihat sebelumnya. Mereka semua sama sepertiku. Beberapa dari mereka melambaikan belalai kecil mereka ke arahku, seolah menyuruhku mendekat. "Ikutlah dengan kami, mari kita jalan bersama." kata salah seorang dari mereka sambil tersenyum.

"Maaf, kalian semua siapa? Kenapa mirip sepertiku?" Tanyaku bingung.

"Kami adalah nenek buyutmu yang lahir dan mati jauh sebelum kamu dilahirkan. Ayo pergi bersama ke tempat yang lebih terang dan indah. Di mana hanya ada bangsa kita dengan hamparan hutan nan luas, sungai yang bersih dan tentunya tidak akan ada makhluk berkaki dua." Jawab mereka menjelaskan.

Aku hanya tersenyum mengangguk pada mereka. Entah kenapa aku sangat senang dan langkahku terasa ringan menuju cahaya yang terang.

Tata the Tapir 🌸

Written by Anis

When I opened my eyes, I saw a large creature in front of me. I didn't know what it was. Then she whispered softly beside me and said, "Welcome to the world, my dear child." It turned out she was my mother. I didn't know how I could understand what she was saying. It felt as if I had just woken up from a very long sleep. Shortly after, she gently helped me stand. I felt very weak and could fall at any moment. But my mother slowly began guiding me to walk.

Slowly but surely, I was finally able to walk. It felt much better than when I first stood up. My mother was always by my side and kept talking to me. She said that other children usually take longer to walk after being born.

"You are indeed my amazing child. Every day, for over a year while you were in my womb, I prayed for you to be a healthy and strong child."

Not long after, Mother told me to drink and nurse from her. It’s hard to describe, but I liked it and wanted to keep nursing because I felt hungry and thirsty.

"Mother, am I really your child?" I asked in between my walking lessons.

"Of course, why would you ask that?" my mother replied.

"Why does my skin color look so different from yours?" I asked, having been curious for a while because my massive mother had dark skin on her front half and a lighter color on her back half. Meanwhile, I also had dark and light skin, but my pattern was filled with tight stripes and spots.

"As you grow older, your skin will go through many changes. And later, when you're an adult, you will have the same pattern as I do," Mother answered with a smile.

"Is that true, Mother?" I asked, my curiosity piqued again.

"I could never lie to my sweet child." She smiled, then suddenly looked at me cheerfully. "I thought of a fitting name for you, my child. Tata. Very sweet, isn't it?"

"I love it, Mom," I said with a smile.

"From now on, we will always be together, okay? Don't wander too far from me, Tata. Understand?"

"Yes. I understand," I said, nodding.

Days turned into weeks. My mother tirelessly taught me all sorts of things. From choosing which leaves were edible to teaching me how to swim in shallow rivers. We explored the forest, and she showed me which areas I should avoid because they were dangerous. Besides the threat of wild beasts, Mother said there were two-legged creatures often seen in the forest. Mother told me that these two-legged creatures were not as ferocious as other predators, at least not to our kind. Therefore, she advised me that if I ever crossed paths with them, I should just walk slowly and ignore them. "We won't be bothered if we don't bother them." I just nodded every time Mother told me or taught me something. I didn't think much of it. After all, I would always be with my mother. *Surely Mother will be the one to face the two-legged creatures and stay by my side,* I thought.

Until the day came when Mother said it was time for me to find a mate and a new territory for my future family. At first, it felt incredibly hard to part with my mother. But for some reason, there was another urge inside me pushing me to discover and explore the forest deeper. I said my goodbyes to my mother, who looked increasingly frail with age. She didn't forget to pray for me and reminded me to always be careful and vigilant in every step I took.

For days, I roamed through parts of the forest I had never entered before. But I hadn't met another creature like me. Until, from a distance, I heard a strange noise I had never heard before. I approached the source of the sound, getting closer and closer, and suddenly I found myself at the edge of the forest. The dirt path I had been treading on softly suddenly turned into hard ground, like stone. "It seems I'm lost and took the wrong path. I should head back."

But as I was about to turn around, I saw a box-like object rolling closer and stopping not far from where I stood. Two two-legged creatures stepped out of it. I remembered my mother's advice to stay calm and just ignore them if we ever met. As I was about to head back into the forest, suddenly many other box-like objects appeared, followed by many more two-legged creatures. I, who was initially calm, now became quite frightened as they swarmed around me. Some of them held small box-like objects and pointed them at me, as if looking at me through them. I was confused. My legs went weak, just like when I was first learning to walk. Eventually, I sat down on the hard ground for a while. After that, I saw one two-legged creature driving the others away one by one until they were quite far from where I was resting.

Seeing this, I didn't waste the chance to escape. I stood up and started walking away from the crowd of two-legged creatures. No one followed me. It seemed Mother was right about them not being cruel, especially to creatures like us. I was relieved, thank goodness.

I walked quite a distance until I finally returned to a dense part of the forest, thick with trees. I tried exploring every corner of it. I felt I was now very far from where the two-legged creatures were. I satisfied my hunger with various forest leaves. Not long after, I heard footsteps slowly approaching me. Just as I was about to run, sensing a threatening presence, a long stick pierced through my stomach. I immediately collapsed, an excruciating pain spreading throughout my entire body. I saw several of the two-legged creatures I had seen earlier running towards me as I lay dying. I tried with all my might to get up and escape, but I fell again, and the ever-growing pain tortured me even more. I surrendered when I saw so much blood pouring from my stomach. *Is this the end of my life?* That thought grew stronger when I saw a two-legged creature holding some kind of long metal object that looked capable of slashing anything in its path. I closed my eyes amidst the agonizing pain. "Forgive me, for it turns out I wasn't strong enough to live independently without you, Mother."

**Epilogue**

I saw a creature just like me lying helplessly in the middle of the two-legged creatures. Wait a minute. *Isn't that me? Am I not already dead? Why am I still awake and able to stand again?* But my legs felt weak. *Can't they see me? Why are they ignoring "me"?* For a moment, I wrestled with my own thoughts. It seemed it was true that I was dead. And the "me" right now was probably some kind of spirit detached from "Tata's" body.

I could see how the two-legged creatures began to cut and slice parts of my body. They occasionally laughed very loudly, though I couldn't fathom what was so funny about chopping up another creature's body. They even did it with expressions completely devoid of guilt. I saw someone holding a strange small box-like object and start speaking into it. Then, they put small pieces of my body into a pot of boiling water. Only then did I realize that this place was very different from where I had last collapsed.

I could no longer bear to watch all of this. It turned out Mother was wrong. The two-legged creatures were incredibly cruel and greedy. They even tortured me before truly killing me. They felt immense joy in the cruelty they consciously committed. So greedy that they consumed my entire body, leaving nothing behind.

I walked slowly, not knowing where I was heading. A bright light at the end of the path was so dazzling. Without realizing it, I stepped towards where the light originated. From a distance, I saw faces that were similar yet seemed unfamiliar, as if I had never seen them before. They were all just like me. Some of them waved their short trunks at me, as if beckoning me closer. "Come with us, let's walk together," one of them said with a smile.

"Excuse me, who are all of you? Why do you look like me?" I asked, confused.

"We are your ancestors who were born and died long before you were even born. Let's go together to a brighter and more beautiful place. Where there is only our kind, with vast expanses of forest, clean rivers, and certainly, no two-legged creatures," they explained.

I just smiled and nodded at them. For some reason, I felt incredibly happy, and my steps felt light as I walked toward the bright light.
